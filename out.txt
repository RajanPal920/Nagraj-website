Legal and Ethical Considerations
Before scraping, we check robots.txt (it likely allows crawling since the sitemap is provided) and any 
Terms of Use on textronsteelalloys.com. Our scraper identifies itself with a user-agent and uses polite
delays to avoid overloading the server【74†L1702-L1710】. We only scrape publicly available product
information (no login/auth needed). No personal or private data is targeted. Scraped data is used for
legitimate purposes like analysis or import; we acknowledge that heavy scraping can impose load, so
rate-limits and respectfulness are paramount. 
Sources: Textron Steel & Alloys sitemap and product pages (primary content); Scrapy documentation on
SitemapSpider and robots.txt【39†L857-L865】【74†L1702-L1710】; Wikipedia on sitemaps【30†L331-
L339】. These informed the scraper design and guidelines.
